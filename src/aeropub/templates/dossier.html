A supplement expiring publishes nothing. A NOTAM lapsing publishes nothing.

How this is being published 

    
ICAO PANS-AIM: information expected to persist beyond three months belongs
      in an AIP Supplement or Amendment, not a NOTAM. These are measurements against that
      standard, not a compliance judgement.
